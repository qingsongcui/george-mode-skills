# Resuming from the right checkpoint

1. Locate this task's existing checkpoint/session/branch; read metadata, recent messages and key decisions first; keep the original goal and all later corrections. Default to the current task scope only.
2. Check the live workspace, head/base, diff, landed content, uncommitted changes, processes, platform state and active owners. Historical reports are leads; real evidence decides whether something is done.
3. Compare done vs pending and reuse still-valid evidence; re-verify only drift, failure or absence; do not redo from scratch, and above all do not repeat unknown external operations.
4. Restore un-acked inbox messages, gates, frontier and current verdicts; a terminated process does not mean the artifact is missing—look for the artifact before deciding to redispatch.
5. State the resumption point and enter the matching playbook; resumption is not an independent endless audit. At the end, say what was inherited, what was re-verified and what was completed.
