## 1. How this system should be positioned

*Reflexive Practice: Private Canon* is best positioned as an integrated practice framework with explicit value constraints, revisable models and a continuous dossier. It puts world recognition, actor conditions, value goals, interaction interfaces, causal identification, candidate actions, sequential commitment, risk control and feedback learning into one decision process.

The system has already been used alongside real projects; such experience is valuable for finding problems, forming candidate rules and delimiting where it applies. Claiming further that it is generally superior to other methods still requires fixing versions, declaring comparison baselines, counting analysis costs and accumulating traceable evidence. Personal practical experience should not be erased, nor can it directly replace cross-context validation.

The way of integrating and the organization of terms may be personal. Its component ideas are clearly connected to existing research on bounded rationality, system feedback, reflexivity, double-loop learning, effectuation logic and causal inference. This document does not claim to establish new natural laws, nor does it judge practical usefulness on the basis of terminological similarity.

## 2. Retained practical skeleton

- Actor, position, world and values are interrelated; one cannot analyze only the external environment.
- Interaction interfaces are used to find channels through which influence can actually be exerted, and allow admitting current unreachability.
- Atomized action, probes, building momentum, staged commitment, doubling down and withdrawal are kept intact.
- Emphasis on multi-actor responses, feedback delays, dependencies, risk reversal and future optionality.
- The theory may be revised based on experience, but revisions carry a duty to record and explain.
- Every new case defaults to full analysis; a module may be judged not applicable with a basis, but content must not be invented to fill a format.

## 4. Levels of update

An update first applies at the appropriate level that can explain the current evidence: record errors correct the original data; execution deviations change execution arrangements; environmental changes revise conditions of application; counter-evidence to a mechanism adjusts the model; value changes form a new goal version; only cross-case evidence may support general rules and theory adjustments.

There is no rule that "after some number of successes the theory is automatically upgraded". Record independence, baseline performance, failures and differences in applicability matter more than mechanical counting. Keeping things unchanged is also an update outcome that needs a reason and a next test.

## 6. What still needs testing in use

1. Whether the full process, compared with a simplified process, reduces key omissions enough to offset reading, analysis and maintenance time.
2. In which tasks the interaction interface, actor engineering, strategic response and other modules each add real decision value.
3. Whether execution reliability, prediction quality, risk control and long-term value improve after adopting this system; one cannot judge only whether the writing structure is tidy.
4. Whether rules transfer across tasks or environments, and where they fail.
5. Whether the record-keeping system is too heavy: which fields carry information value, and which can be merged without losing key history.

A validation plan should set main metrics, comparison methods, time windows and cost definitions before observing results. When data is insufficient, keep "uncertain", and keep the basis for continued use separate from claims that the method has been proven superior.

## 7. Delivery checks and actual boundaries

The theory text keeps the general outline, twenty volumes and appendices; the operating templates cover new-case analysis, plans, execution, observation, review, update, closing, rules and method validation. The record templates contain no fictional real business results.

The workbook was verified with real input changes using the generation tool's calculation engine, checking blanks and zeros, budget totals, reference anomalies, review due dates, probability bounds, prediction scoring and some missing closing items, and each sheet's display was checked. Specific capabilities and limits are in the field specification. Operational verification in the user's local Excel or WPS has not been completed.

The original helper tools (not included in this public edition) do not automatically update the model or theory; semantic judgments and the basis for adoption are made by the user and the collaborating AI from actual materials.
